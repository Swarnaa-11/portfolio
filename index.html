Hi, I'm Swarna. I design clear, human interfaces, and I like making complicated things feel simple. I start with people rather than screens, then design, code and test until it feels obvious.

I'm a communication designer turned UX designer, currently studying for an MA in User Experience Design at London College of Communication. I've worked in agencies and for brands across the UK, US and India, from identity and social to full websites.
